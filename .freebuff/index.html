// 07 — FAQ

Asked often.

Will all this WebGL tank my performance budget?

No. The scene auto-detects device capability, caps DPR at 2, and offers a quality toggle. On weak GPUs it degrades to a pure-CSS aurora — same brand, zero frames dropped.

Who owns the code and the generative systems?

You do. Every engagement ships with a private repo, documented shader library, and an Artifacts license for the procedural tools we build for you.

Can you integrate with our existing stack?

Yes — we regularly embed scenes into Next.js, Astro, Shopify Hydrogen and plain CMS sites. The renderer is framework-agnostic and mounts into any container.

How do revisions work?

Each sprint includes weekly demo builds with asynchronous feedback windows. Two structured revision rounds per milestone are included in every plan.
